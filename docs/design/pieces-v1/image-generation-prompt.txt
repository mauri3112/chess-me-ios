Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: original 2D chess piece family and matching chessboard, a precise visual design proposal for a native iOS chess learning app.
Primary request: Create one polished, restrained design sheet showing a complete new original set of chess pieces on a matching board, plus a clear specimen lineup of all six white and all six black pieces. The art must be suitable as the base for real app piece sprites. Familiar minimal Staunton silhouettes comparable in simplicity and legibility to major online chess sites, but draw original contours, do not copy Lichess Cburnett or Chess.com Neo artwork.

Composition: square image, around 2048x2048. Warm paper background #F5F2EA. Upper 70 percent: one large perfectly frontal flat 8 by 8 chessboard, with alternating warm ivory #E9E5DA and muted sage #86A391 squares, evenly sized, no perspective or 3D. Standard initial position, white at the bottom. White bottom rank from left to right: rook knight bishop queen king bishop knight rook. Exactly eight white pawns on second rank. Exactly eight black pawns on seventh rank. Black top rank from left to right: rook knight bishop queen king bishop knight rook. All pieces upright facing the viewer, both knight colors show the same left-facing profile. Lower part: two neat aligned horizontal specimen rows, white then black, six pieces in each row ordered KING QUEEN BISHOP KNIGHT ROOK PAWN. Each column has the same invisible square canvas; every piece baseline aligned. Under bottom row, tiny neat labels King Queen Bishop Knight Rook Pawn, no other words, no logo.

Geometry and scale are critical: visible silhouette height as percent of ONE board square: king 82%, queen 80%, bishop 79%, knight 78%, rook 72%, pawn 68%. Equivalent relative to king: 100,98,96,95,88,83 percent. Do NOT make the pawns tiny or the knights enormous. White and black versions of each piece have exactly the same silhouette and dimensions. All have generous consistent square padding and the same shared baseline. Piece width: king about 68%, queen 72%, bishop 60%, knight 64%, rook 60%, pawn 52% of square. One consistent line weight everywhere, 2.4% of square.

Design: crisp vector-like 2D artwork, rounded joins, solid fills, clean well-proportioned traditional recognizable shapes. All six pieces share a restrained wide shallow rounded plinth, one collar line, smoothly tapered bodies, no ornate carving. King recognizable simple cross above a rounded shoulder crown and tapered body. Queen recognizable five-point crown with small rounded tips, broad crown and slim neck. Bishop recognizable classic pointed mitre with diagonal slit and small finial. Knight an elegant clean horse profile with short orderly angular mane, one small round eye, distinguishable muzzle, sculpted neck into common base. Rook strong simple tower with three squared crenellations, straight shoulders and slight tapered stem. Pawn round head, simple collar, gently tapered body, smaller shared plinth. No letters inside pieces and no Unicode font chess symbols. Identical contours in both colors.
Colors: white pieces warm porcelain #FAF7EF with clean dark evergreen #25352E contour and minimal contour details. Black pieces deep evergreen charcoal #293D33, narrow contrasting soft pale-sage #BAC8B7 internal details so the knight eye, bishop slit, collar, and base remain readable. Black outer contours dark #1D2B24. At most one flat inset tone per piece, absolutely no glossy gradients or perspective rendering. Matching soft green/ivory chessboard should keep both armies instantly readable on both square colors.
Constraints: no photorealistic physical chess, no perspective, no 3D, no shadows behind individual pieces, no wood grain, no noisy texture, no inconsistent widths or line weights, no resizing individual pieces to fill their square, no app chrome, no marketing headline. Board geometry must be exactly 8 columns and 8 rows. Keep repeated pieces perfectly consistent. The result should look like a carefully drawn usable original app asset family, understated and familiar.
